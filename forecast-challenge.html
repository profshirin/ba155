Forecast Challenge

You manage Campus Corner Café. Pumpkin spice lattes have been flying off the counter for 8 weeks. Now you must tell the owner how many to plan for next week. Order too few and fans leave angry. Order too many and pumpkin sauce goes to waste. The closest forecast in the class wins bragging rights.
