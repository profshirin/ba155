The Beer Game

Four companies pass cases of root beer down a supply line, from the factory to the distributor, to the wholesaler, to the retailer, and finally to customers. You run one of them. The computer runs the other three. Your goal is to keep your team's total cost as low as possible for 20 weeks.

The catch? Orders and shipments take time to travel, and no one can see what anyone else is doing.

$0.50per case in your inventory, every week
$1.00per case on backorder, every week. Customers waiting cost more!
3 weeksfrom placing an order until the cases arrive. One week for the order to travel, two weeks for shipping.
20 weeksin a round. Everyone starts with 12 cases and steady orders of 4.
