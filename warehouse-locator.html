Where Should the Warehouse Go?

Pearl Tea Café is growing. It now has five stores across Southern California, and the owner wants one warehouse to supply them all. Your job is to find the best spot. You will use the center of gravity method, visit the spot on Google Maps, and see what happens when the map changes.

Have a calculator or Excel open. This is a light week, and the sim takes about 30 minutes.

Part 1 of 3 · Do the math

Find the center of gravity

Latitude tells you how far north a place is. Longitude tells you how far east or west. In Southern California, longitude is negative, so keep the minus sign!

The stores

Volume is cases shipped to each store per week.

Your answer, to 4 decimal places

Center latitude = sum of (latitude × volume) ÷ total volume. Center longitude = sum of (longitude × volume) ÷ total volume.
