Pearl Stock

You run the stockroom at Pearl Tea Café. Every week you decide how many bags of tapioca pearls to order. Order too little and customers leave without their boba. Order too much and pearls sit on the shelf, costing money and going stale. Run the stockroom for 10 weeks at the lowest total cost.

About 50 bagsCustomers use about 50 bags a week, but it swings between about 35 and 65.
$100 per orderEach delivery costs $100, no matter how many bags.
$1 per bagEach bag left on the shelf at the end of a week costs $1 to store, chill, and replace when it goes stale.
$5 per bag shortEach bag you are short means lost drinks and unhappy customers.
1 weekAn order you place arrives at the start of next week. You start with 60 bags.
Hint from Chapter 8. Find the EOQ first. Annual demand is about 50 × 52 = 2,600 bags. Each order costs $100. Holding one bag for a year costs $1 × 52 = $52. Then think about a reorder point with some safety stock.

You get one 10 week run. The lowest total cost in the class wins.
