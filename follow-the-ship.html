Follow the ship

Right now, giant container ships are lining up off the coast near Cerritos, waiting to unload at the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach. In this sim you will track one real ship across the ocean, then face the Incoterms showdown. Every right answer counts, and the clock breaks ties. The top score in the class wins!

  1. Track a real ship. Find a container ship headed to LA or Long Beach on a free ship tracker and log its trip.
  2. Incoterms showdown. 16 questions. Show where risk passes from seller to buyer, then pick the right term for real deals.
  3. Get your code. Copy your ship log and result code into the Sim 7 template.
Before you start. Review the Incoterms table in the Week 7 study notes. You get one try at the showdown, and the clock keeps running even if you close the page.
Part 1 of 2

Track a real ship

How to find your ship

  1. Open VesselFinder and zoom in on Los Angeles and Long Beach. The ships waiting offshore are a great place to look.
  2. Click a ship icon. Pick one whose type says Container Ship and whose destination is Los Angeles or Long Beach. A ship that just arrived also works.
  3. Click the ship's name to open its details page. Look for the flag, the last port with its departure date (ATD), and the ETA.

Can't find one? Try MarineTraffic or MyShipTracking instead. Every student should pick their own ship.
