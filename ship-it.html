Part 1 of 2

Get real quotes

How to get your quotes

  1. Open each calculator. USPS · UPS · FedEx
  2. Ship from ZIP 90703 to 95814 (Sacramento), 60601 (Chicago), and 10001 (New York). Use a box of 12 by 10 by 8 inches that weighs 5 pounds.
  3. Pick the ground service. That is USPS Ground Advantage, UPS Ground, and FedEx Ground or FedEx Home Delivery. Skip extras like insurance or pickup.
  4. Type the price and the number of business days for each one below. No login is needed.
