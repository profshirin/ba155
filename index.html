Logistics simulations

A set of short, browser based games that let students practice real logistics decisions, from managing a supply chain and choosing suppliers to forecasting demand, setting inventory, locating a warehouse, and shipping across borders. Each one ties directly to a chapter of Contemporary Logistics by Murphy and Knemeyer and to the course student learning outcomes.

Free and openNo logins, apps, or textbook codes. Works on laptops and phones.
30 to 40 minutesEach sim is short and focused on one or two core ideas.
Real world settingsLocal examples like a Cerritos boba shop and the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach.
Built in integrityOne play per student and a unique result code the instructor can verify and rank.

How they work in the course

  • Students open each sim from a Canvas assignment, play it, and copy their results and a short reflection into a Word template.
  • Most sims produce a result code tied to the student's name. A built in instructor page checks every code and builds a class leaderboard in seconds.
  • Grading rewards participation and reflection, not luck. Leaderboards add friendly competition and a weekly shout out.
  • Students build on these skills in a term long project, where they plan the global launch of a real company's product.

Trying them out

Feel free to play any simulation. Each one allows one play per browser so students cannot replay for a better score. To look around more than once, open the page in a private or incognito window.
