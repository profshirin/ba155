Logistics simulations

Hi, I'm Shirin! I teach BA 155 at Cerritos College, and I made these little games so my students could try logistics instead of just reading about it. Each one takes about half an hour and runs right in the browser, on a laptop or a phone. There is nothing to download and no login.

Why I built them

Logistics can feel abstract on the page. Things like the bullwhip effect or EOQ start to make sense when you are the one running out of tapioca pearls or watching factory orders spiral. So I set most of the games close to home, at a boba shop in Cerritos or a container ship heading into Long Beach, where students can picture what is going on.

Students open each game from Canvas, play it once, and write a short reflection about what happened. Most of the games give them a result code, which I can check on a teacher page that also builds a class leaderboard. Their grade comes from taking part and thinking it through, not from the score, but the leaderboard adds a bit of friendly competition.
