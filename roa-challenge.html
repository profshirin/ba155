The ROA Challenge

Congratulations, you are the new VP of Logistics at Pearl Tea Co., a company that sells boba tea kits to stores across the U.S. The board is not happy. Return on assets is stuck at 20%, and they want at least 26% by next year. You have budget for exactly three logistics projects. Choose wisely. You only get one shot.

How you score

  • ROA points. Your final ROA × 10. An ROA of 24.5% earns 245 points.
  • Prediction points. Before you see results, predict how each project would move margin and turnover on its own. 15 points for each correct call, up to 90.
  • Board bonus. 50 points if you hit ROA of 26% or more with a fill rate of 95% or more.
  • Service penalty. Minus 50 points if your fill rate falls below 95%. Every point below 95% also costs you 3% of sales, because stores buy elsewhere when you run out.

Watch out. Some projects help each other, and some make each other worse.
